SQL used for verification:
  SELECT r.current_tier,
         COUNT(*) AS jobs,
         ROUND(100.0*COUNT(*)/SUM(COUNT(*)) OVER(),2) AS pct,
         ROUND(AVG(ps.priority_score),4) AS avg_score,
         MIN(ps.priority_score) AS min_score,
         MAX(ps.priority_score) AS max_score,
         ps.scoring_method, ps.feature_version_id
  FROM rpotential.jobs j
  JOIN rpotential.job_routing r          ON r.job_id    = j.job_id
  JOIN rpotential.job_priority_scores ps ON ps.score_id = r.last_score_id
  WHERE j.upload_id = 1
    AND j.date_added >= '2025-12-01'
    AND j.date_added <  '2025-12-31'
  GROUP BY r.current_tier, ps.scoring_method, ps.feature_version_id
  ORDER BY r.current_tier;

Key takeaways:
  1. Blended weights moved 18 jobs out of T3 and into T2 (+17) and T1 (+1).
     All 17 T2 additions were Placed jobs — correctly identified placements
     that the heuristic left in T3.

  2. T1 fill rate dropped slightly (76.9% → 71.4%) because one new job
     (Service Desk, job 1607875) entered T1 with Administrative Close outcome.
     With only 13/14 T1 jobs, a single miss moves the fill rate noticeably.

  3. T2 fill rate improved (84.5% → 85.6%): the 17 additional T2 jobs were
     all Placed, making T2 marginally more precise.

  4. T3 Placed count fell from 608 → 591 (-17), meaning 17 fewer placements
     are buried in the deprioritised queue — a direct reduction in missed
     opportunities.

  5. The AUC-ROC on this December validation set = 0.8828 (from train_weights.py),
     vs train AUC 0.8558.  Gap = 0.0271 — well within the ≤0.05 overfit
     threshold. The model generalises correctly to December data.
