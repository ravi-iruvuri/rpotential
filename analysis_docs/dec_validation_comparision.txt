TAKEAWAYS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  1. Blended weights moved 18 jobs from T3 → T2/T1. All 17 that went to T2
     were Placed jobs — genuine placements the heuristic buried in T3.

  2. T2 fill rate improved 84.5% → 85.6%: every additional T2 job was a
     real placement, making T2 marginally more precise.

  3. T1 fill rate dipped 76.9% → 71.4% due to one borderline Service Desk
     job (job 1607875) entering T1 with Administrative Close outcome.
     With only 13/14 T1 jobs, one miss moves the rate noticeably — this
     is not a systematic model failure.

  4. The dominant score driver in both tests is the company component.
     Blended weights amplified it (0.45→0.50), which is consistent with
     the logistic regression finding that company fill rate is the strongest
     predictor (learned weight 0.66 vs heuristic 0.45).

  5. T3 still holds 591 Placed jobs (31.9% of December T3). This is not
     a model failure — it reflects that many jobs at low-fill-rate companies
     genuinely do get placed, but the model cannot predict which ones without
     richer features (e.g. recruiter experience, candidate pipeline depth).
     This is the addressable gap for Phase 3 (probability-based scoring).
